# Working on Towns and Peasants

## UI rules (from the owner; these override habit)

- **Simplicity is the guiding light.** Do one thing well and nothing more. UI elements must never detract from the game.
- **Trust the player. Don't hold their hand.** No explanations of why something can't be done, no hints, no
  "Roll first" or "Not your turn". If an action isn't available, its button simply isn't there.
- **The UI is not a conversation.** No sentences, no "you/your/their", no chatty phrases ("Nobody wants it: take it
  back", "Your offer to everyone"). Use an icon, a single word, or two or three words at most ("Trade pending", "Move
  robber").
- **No labels the picture already says.** If arrows or placement make it clear, drop the words (the trade tray has
  no "You get / You give").
- **Small and out of the way.** Prefer a chip, a mark or a glow over a panel. Never cover the board for status.
- **Legible pictures.** Icons and marks on the table must be big enough to read at a glance on a phone.

## Other standing rules

- The game, its card names, art and rules text are our own; don't copy the published game's names, art or text.
- Commit messages and code carry no model identifiers.
